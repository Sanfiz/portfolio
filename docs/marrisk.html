MarRISK — Coastal Risk Assessment for Northwest Iberia

🌊 MeteoGalicia 📍 Santiago de Compostela, Spain 📅 2018 – 2021 💶 Budget: €3.5M

MarRISK was a European Interreg POCTEP project focused on climate change adaptation for the coastlines of Galicia and Northern Portugal. The goal was to evaluate the most significant coastal risks under future climate scenarios — flooding, extreme events, toxic algal blooms, coastal erosion — and improve the resilience of traditional economic sectors and emerging industries such as marine renewable energy.

I joined the project as a climate scientist at MeteoGalicia, the regional meteorological service of Galicia, where I worked on the oceanographic and climate components of the project. My work involved processing large volumes of model output and observational buoy data to characterise the current and projected coastal climate of the region.

MarRISK brought together researchers, public authorities and stakeholders from both sides of the Spanish-Portuguese border. That cross-border collaboration — coordinating with partners in Vigo, Porto and Lisbon, communicating results to non-scientific audiences — was as valuable as the technical work itself.

What I did

  • Processed outputs from oceanographic models and observational buoy networks along the Galician and Northern Portuguese coast
  • Conducted regional climate analysis to characterise present and projected future conditions
  • Prepared scientific and technical reports on coastal climate risks
  • Contributed to stakeholder communication and project dissemination materials
